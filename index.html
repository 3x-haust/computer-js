Computer.js

Your Mac's real capabilities, from a website. Native menu-bar app, control panel, and SDK — built in Swift.

Download DMG GitHub

Get started

  1. Download and install Computer.js Runtime.app from the DMG (drag to Applications)
  2. Launch it — opens a control panel at http://127.0.0.1:8788
  3. Grant permissions when prompted — your Mac is now connected
